Jam Radio v2: design and parts

Revised 2026-11-27: no handle (it lives plugged into the wall), full-height speaker chambers, a 144 mm centre bay, a centred 32 mm slot, sled on rails, power in the back panel, and the arched 5×8 LED dial. Drawn to scale (mm) from docs/enclosure.md. The LEDs show the jukebox equaliser mid-song.

Design

Key numbers

  • 360 × 170 × 200 mm12 mm Baltic birch, glued butt joints with glue blocks, 10 mm roundovers, 4 screw-on feet
  • Slot 32 × 84 mmStepped insert from a ½" HDPE cutting board, 45° lip, no metal within 30 mm of the reader
  • Cartridge 80 × 100 × 30 mmSolid maple, a sealed tag under each face, printed label in a 1 mm recess
  • Read gap ~5–7 mmNear tag 1.5 mm deep + 0–2 mm clearance + 3 mm pocketed wall, either way round
  • Chambers ≈ 1.0 L each90 × 176 × 64 mm, sealed, drivers 246 mm apart behind 84 mm drilled grilles
  • Buttons 24 mmMini LED arcade buttons, 40 mm apart, nuts threadlocked; bay 144 mm
  • LED window 52 × 84 mmArch above the grid, maple veneer at the front face
  • Airflow ≈ 20 cm² in / outUnder the sled and low on the cover, out high on the back
