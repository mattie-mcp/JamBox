Jam Radio: design review

To-scale drawings, a rendered look, and a tolerance check of the chosen enclosure, so you can critique the look, proportions and fits before cutting wood. Numbers come from docs/enclosure.md and docs/hardware.md, updated with the 2026-11-27 decisions (5×8 portrait dial, 44 mm knob, 19 mm power button, vent plan).

Biggest clash

The handle dowel sits right over the slot. A cartridge can't drop straight in, and with the cut-list 70 mm uprights the proud cartridge hits it.

Cartridge fit

19 + 12 mm lamination makes a 31 mm block with the tag off-centre: ≈1.2 mm clearance per side and a 20 or 27 mm read gap (target ≤20).

Front crowding

The 30 mm PN532 rule, read vertically, pushes the window low and the buttons almost onto the bottom edge. The arch then hides 2 LEDs and clips 2.

Table score

27 fits checked: 10 flagged, 8 tight, 9 OK. 13 conflicts between the docs.

4. Critique and suggested adjustments

In priority order. Tags: lookfitsafetybuild

  1. fitsafetyThe handle blocks the cartridge. The dowel runs straight over the slot. A 100 mm cartridge can't drop in vertically under a dowel only ≈71.5 mm up, and with the cut-list 70 mm uprights the proud cartridge would hit it.
    Suggest: Move the slot 10 mm forward (centre at y = 75) and the dowel back to y = 130. That leaves ≈27 mm between the cartridge's back face and the dowel. Keep the dowel ≥70 mm up so an adult hand fits.
  2. fitlookThe centre bay front is overcrowded. Reading the 30 mm PN532 rule as a vertical offset puts the window at z 54–118, the buttons at z 30, and leaves a 4 mm flat above the bottom roundover. The button nuts also hit the chamber floors and the sled.
    Suggest: Treat the 30 mm as 3D clearance: the LED shield is already ≈75 mm in front of the antenna. Then centre the window on the drivers. Also consider +20 mm height (360 × 220, close to the golden ratio), which costs almost nothing.
  3. lookThe arch eats the equaliser. A semicircular top on a 48 mm window hides 2 corner LEDs and clips 2 more: the peak dots of the bass and treble bands disappear.
    Suggest: Make the window ≈52 × 84 with the arch above the LED field. Let the crown glow as a plain amber "dial lamp". Size it after measuring the real pitch.
  4. lookfitBring the diffuser to the face. Veneer on the back of a 12 mm panel plus a 6 mm bezel is an 18 mm tunnel. A seated toddler looks down ≈30°, so the top row (the peak dots) is shaded by ≈10 mm.
    Suggest: Glue the veneer into a rebate at the front of the window and chamfer the bezel edge 45°.
  5. safetyReplace the stick-on feet. Jamie peels stickers, and 20 mm rubber feet are small parts.
    Suggest: Glue and screw two hardwood runners (≈28 × 12 mm, full depth) from inside. They read as a radio plinth and keep the 10 mm vent gap.
  6. lookfitShrink the grilles to Ø96–100 and ring them. Ø115 overruns the 110 mm chamber and comes within 8 mm of the roundover.
    Suggest: Use Ø96 hole fields inside turned or router-cut maple rings (≈110 OD). Rings echo the arch bezel and give the classic cathedral-radio face.
  7. safetyfitKnob, upright and handle loads. The knob sits 8 mm from the right upright: a finger gap next to a turning part. A 6 mm mortise with screws in a 12 mm top is a weak root for a 20 kg hang.
    Suggest: Mount the uprights on the outside of the end panels (two bolts each, into the 12 mm sides). It's stronger, frees the top for the knob, and looks like a real portable.
  8. buildfitFix the sled and service-cover path. The sled can't slide over the opening's sill, the outlet sits ≈46 mm below the top (a warm pocket), and the 10 mm USB-C recess won't fit a 6 mm cover.
    Suggest: Raise the sled on 12 mm runners, shorten it to ≈95 mm, make the opening 260 × 150 with its top ≈15 mm under the top panel, and glue a 12 mm boss behind the USB-C.
  9. fitbuildMake cartridges exactly 30 mm with the tag centred. 19 + 12 mm makes 31 (or 31.75) mm, leaves ≈1.2 mm per side, and puts the tag 12 or 19 mm from a face (gap 20 or 27 mm).
    Suggest: Resaw or plane ¾″ stock to two 15 mm halves. Batch it with a planer stop. Then the "either way round" promise holds.
  10. buildRecompute the cut list for the joint you'll cut. The cut list is butt-joint sizes but the build says rabbets. Plywood is under nominal.
    Suggest: Cut a test rabbet in the real stock, make a story stick, then size the sides and top/bottom (+12 mm for 6 mm rabbets).
  11. lookCheap upgrades toward a classic radio. Natural birch alone reads as a nice box rather than a radio.
    Suggest: Paint an inset fascia (milk paint, deep teal or cream) with natural maple rings and bezel; round the vertical corners harder (12–15 mm) than the face edges; add a routed "JAM" badge, or a single painted "station strip" under the window (no metal near the antenna).
  12. safetybuildKeep the slot area metal-free and spill-proof. HDPE screws at the insert's back corners land ≈29 mm from the antenna. The drain must exit the box, which here means through the service cover.
    Suggest: Use nylon screws, a push-fit silicone drain tube to a Ø10 hole in the cover, and run the spill test before the final glue-up.

5. Conflicts between the docs

Where these disagree, the drawings follow the 2026-11-27 decisions.

  • Handle height: +100 mm in the size table, concept drawing and "≈310 mm to the top", but the cut list uprights are 70 mm (≈64 mm above the top after a 6 mm mortise).
  • Button hole: cut list says "usually 28 mm"; hardware.md hole-cutting says a 30 mm Forstner.
  • Read gap: slot design gives ≈23 mm; hardware.md §5 sets a design target of ≤20 mm.
  • Cartridge thickness and tag position: spec is 30 mm with the tag at the centre; the recipe is 19 + 12 mm with the tag at the glue line (31 mm thick, tag 12/19 mm from the faces).
  • Presence sensing: the cartridge table says "Presence is sensed by a microswitch"; the decision log says NFC-only + detent.
  • Centre bay width: "~100 mm"; geometry gives 104 mm if the box sides are the chambers' outer walls, or 88 mm if each chamber is a separate box centred on its driver (then the 95 mm sled doesn't fit).
  • Grille vs chamber: Ø115 grille vs 110 mm chamber.
  • USB-C recess: 10 mm-deep pocket (Concept A, hardware.md E6) vs the Radio's 6 mm service cover.
  • Roundover: 3/8″ on the front (cut list) vs 6 mm everywhere (safety checklist).
  • Joinery: "rabbet joints" (Concept B) vs butt-joint part sizes in the cut list.
  • Slot sleeve depth: slot design still says it hangs 60 mm "into the attic under the ridge cap" (Concept A); the Radio uses 48 mm under a 12 mm top.
  • PN532 board: E4 names the Adafruit #364, which is out of stock; the HiLetgo V3 was bought. The drawings assume the V3 (≈43 × 40 mm), which fits the 96 × 48 sleeve wall.
  • Inside-the-radio diagram shows the old "LED 5×8" between chambers at mid-height, while the window rule (≥30 mm below the PN532) pushes it low. The latest decision is drawn.

6. Open questions for you

  1. Is the 30 mm PN532 clearance meant as 3D distance (the LED shield is ≈75 mm away in depth) or as a vertical offset on the face? This decides the whole front layout.
  2. Handle: 70 mm uprights or a +100 mm handle? Are you open to side-mounted uprights and moving the dowel behind the slot?
  3. Would +20 mm of height (360 × 220) be acceptable?
  4. Arch: full semicircle, or a taller window with the arch above the LED field? Is an unlit or softly lit crown OK?
  5. Which buttons are going in: the Adafruit LED arcade buttons as bought? What's their measured hole and thread length?
  6. Cartridges: plane to 30 mm with two 15 mm halves, or keep 19 + 12 and widen the slot to 36 mm?
  7. Finish: natural birch, or a painted fascia? Any colour you already have in mind?
  8. Feet: OK to swap stick-on rubber feet for screwed hardwood runners?
  9. Where should the crumb drain exit: through the service cover, or a separate hole in the back panel?
  10. Can you measure the NeoPixel Shield's LED pitch (or pull it from Adafruit's board files) before the window is cut?

7. Assumptions made for the drawings

  • Box sides double as the chambers' outer walls (gives the ≈100 mm bay). Chambers are centred vertically (drivers at z = 100) and drivers are 210 mm apart.
  • PN532 is the HiLetgo V3 (≈43 × 40 mm), mounted with its top edge under the top panel.
  • LED pitch 8.5 mm (doc says unverified). Window arch is a full semicircle (r = 24).
  • Arcade button bezel Ø33, body Ø30, nut Ø36, body + switch ≈52 mm deep.
  • Handle: uprights 19 × 40, dowel 240 long centred, dowel centre 16 mm below the upright top, uprights centred in depth over the slot.
  • Knob centred at x = 330 above the right chamber; encoder bushing ≈7 mm, nut + washer ≈3 mm, knob bore ≈15 mm.
  • Service opening centred horizontally at z 22–142 (not specified). Cover 280 × 140 at z 12–152.
  • HDPE insert ≈120 × 70 mm with four corner screws; slot chamfer 4 mm.
  • Pi + Amp4 stack 35 mm (doc), extra-tall header +5 mm, plugs +15 mm. PC83-4 frame ≈83 mm, cutout ≈72–73 mm.
  • Feet Ø20 × 10 mm stick-on rubber, 15 mm in from each corner.
